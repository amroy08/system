💳 Intelligent Fee Collection

Global student search inside the payments desk dynamically aggregates dues, waivers, and collection history for fast transaction handling.

Identifies outstanding fee defaulters, allows standards filtering, and prepares personalized WhatsApp alerts with payment links.

Empowers teachers and supervisors to record lessons taught, trace syllabi schedules, and monitor homework compliance.
